# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS; static V1. Confirmed in the client brief.

## Users

People in or around Ho Chi Minh City who need a meaningful flower gift for a birthday, graduation, anniversary, love, wedding, or celebration. Their immediate job is to find a suitable real flower sample and begin a conversation with the shop.

## Product Purpose

Tamas Flower Store is a local florist digital storefront: visitors browse flowers, choose a promising sample, and contact the shop to be advised and place the order manually.

## Positioning

Tamas uses the recipient, occasion, colour tone, and budget as the starting point for a flower gift; it is not a self-service checkout catalogue.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from Google, Facebook, or Instagram, often on a phone and sometimes with a time-sensitive need. The primary action is Zalo, Messenger/Facebook, or phone.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- V1 has no account, cart, checkout, payment, inventory, CMS, database, API, or admin.
- Product data is static but must be isolated from UI for a later CMS/API replacement.
- Routes required: home, product listing, product detail, about, contact.
- Product availability, exact price, and delivery cost must not be fabricated.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Tamas Flower Store.
- Voice: romantic, feminine, thoughtful, handcrafted, contemporary, elegant, warm, premium without distance.
- The client explicitly chose a Boutique Florist × Editorial Fashion visual direction, Cormorant Garamond-style display type, and Be Vietnam Pro-style UI type.
- Real Tamas assets have priority over generated imagery.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real Page-sourced assets and inventory: `../TAMAS-FLOWER/02_Photos/`, `../TAMAS-FLOWER/Phase-0.2-asset-register.csv`, and `../TAMAS-FLOWER/Phase-0.2-summary.md`.
- Current Google Site establishes product categories, address, contact channels, and manual consultation flow.
- No verified public reviews, store photography, detailed payment policy, or finished Google Business profile is on hand.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with real flower photography and clear paths to conversation.
2. Treat a product sample as the opening of a consultation, not a fixed retail SKU.
3. Keep mobile contact access immediate without sacrificing a calm browsing experience.
4. Preserve product truth; use placeholders only where the source is genuinely unverified.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic landmarks, visible keyboard focus, responsive touch targets, meaningful alt text, and readable contrast. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
